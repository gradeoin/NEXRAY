About the Fest

Why LUMINUS stands out as a national platform for innovation.

What is LUMINUS?

LUMINUS is RNSIT’s flagship national-level technical festival bringing together students, innovators, and industry experts to compete, collaborate, and create. From cutting-edge hackathons to project expos and startup pitches, it’s where ideas meet impact.

AI & ML Robotics IoT Cybersecurity Gaming & Esports

What to Expect

  • National participation across top institutions
  • 36-hour hackathon, expert-led workshops
  • Cash prizes, goodies, and internship opportunities
  • Interactive expos and cultural evenings

Organizers

Hosted by RNS Institute of Technology with active involvement of all departments: CSE, ISE, ECE, EEE, Mechanical, Civil, MCA and more. Supported by faculty mentors and a strong student organizing committee.

FAQ

Quick answers to common questions.

Who can participate?

Students from any recognized college/university across India. Some workshops are open to the public.

Is cross-college team allowed?

Yes, inter-college teams are allowed unless an event specifies otherwise.

Do I get accommodation?

Limited accommodation may be available on request. Details will be emailed to confirmed participants.

How to pay event fees?

For paid events, you’ll see a secure payment link after selecting the event. Keep the transaction ID for verification.

Will I get a certificate?

Yes, e-certificates will be available for participants and winners. Use the Certificate Generator with your Registration ID.

How do I stay updated?

Check the announcements ticker on the homepage and follow our official social channels.
