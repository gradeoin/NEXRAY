Professional email (name@yourdomain.com)

The pro move is using a custom domain email for trust and deliverability. You’ll set it up in: Stage 5 (emails + templates) and Stage 4 (DNS).

  • MX records route your email to a provider.
  • SPF/DKIM/DMARC prove you’re legit (reduces spam). You’ll configure these.
  • Transactional email (password resets, receipts) ≠ marketing newsletter.
